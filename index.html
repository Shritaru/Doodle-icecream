our story

Born From a Love of Scoops & Scribbles.

It started with a shared sketchbook, a secondhand freezer, and way too much sugar. Doodle Icecream began as two friends who couldn't stop drawing in the margins of their recipe notes — so eventually, the margins became the whole brand.

Chapter 1

A notebook full of made-up flavors and even-more-made-up cartoon mascots.

Chapter 2

We traded straight lines for wobbly ones, and "flavor of the month" for flavors that make you laugh out loud.

Chapter 3

Today, every cup, cone, and wall in the shop is covered in doodles — because ice cream should always feel a little handmade.
